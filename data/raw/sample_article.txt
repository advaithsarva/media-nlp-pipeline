Everyone knows the new transport bill was a disastrous decision.

Sources say the minister ignored his own advisers, and critics claim the plan was
shameful from the start. The mayor, a liar by any measure, told reporters that every
single objection had been considered.

The bill passed on 14 March by 312 votes to 208. It allocates 4.2 billion pounds over
six years and takes effect in April 2027. The Department for Transport published the
full text on its website the following morning.

Studies show that similar schemes elsewhere have failed, and no serious expert would
defend this one.
